Swapped the brake pads on my Volvo XC60. The wrinkle: the rear calipers use an electronic
parking brake, so the pistons won't just wind back by hand — normally you retract them with
Volvo's approved software and interface (VIDA + DiCE), which is a lot of money for a one-off
job. Instead I drove the caliper's parking-brake motor directly off a 9V battery to retract
the piston, swapped the pads, and reset it. Cheap, simple, and it worked. Good reminder that
"you need the dealer tool" often just means "you need the right voltage on the right two pins."
