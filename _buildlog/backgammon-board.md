Made a backgammon board as a gift, and mostly learned every way a 3D print can go sideways:
dialing in z-plane calibration to get a flat, clean box; a tiny X/Y offset between two colored
filaments that threw off the alignment; running out of filament about five layers from the top
on the version I was sure would be the good one; and — despite knowing backgammon well —
printing the wrong number of triangles on the first attempt. It came out in the end. It just
took more tries than I'd like to admit.
